# The fold design

Twelve open questions, more than one page of Needs you holds, in the three
shapes a folded card can take: one whose first paragraph runs past the lines a
card shows, one whose first paragraph fits with options folded away after it,
and short ones that fit and fold nothing.

1. 💬 **OQ-F1: Is a long question cut at its third line?**
   Its first paragraph runs well past the three lines a folded card shows, so
   the card cuts it short and fades its last line, where Show full question
   sits. The rest of this paragraph is background a reader may want and may
   not: who asked, what was tried, which documents settled the parts that are
   settled, and why the part that is left is still open after three drafts. It
   goes on long enough that no wide screen fits it in three lines, and it ends
   with the sentence a reader only sees unfolded: the end of the question,
